## ARTICLE 4: REGISTRATION AND TERMS OF PARTICIPATION

Participation in the Event takes place exclusively in the dedicated area provided on the AIXAM stand.

To take part, visitors must:

- Enter their details:
  - Last name
  - First name
  - Email address
  - Postcode
- Confirm their email address using the verification code sent to them.
- Accept these rules and the applicable privacy policy.
- Access the customisation interface.
- Create their own SKIN EASY design by selecting the graphic elements provided by the Organising Company, in particular:
  - decorative objects;
  - graphic themes;
  - colours;
  - predefined texts.
- Freely position these elements on the visual of the EASY panel.
- Confirm their creation.

Once the creation has been confirmed, the Participant may receive their visual by email in JPEG format at the address given at registration.

Each Participant may make several creations with the same email address during the Event, subject to the proper functioning of the system.

The Organising Company reserves the right to exclude any creation containing words or visuals that are:

- contrary to public order;
- defamatory, insulting or discriminatory;
- violent, political or religious in nature;
- infringing the rights of third parties.

## ARTICLE 5: SELECTION OF CREATIONS

From all the creations made during the Event, the Organising Company may select one or more creations for study as part of the future development of the SKIN EASY range.

The creations will be assessed by a jury made up of representatives of the Organising Company according to the following criteria:

- Originality of the design;
- Graphic consistency;
- Technical and industrial feasibility;
- Consistency with the EASY brand universe.

This selection does not constitute a commitment to produce or market the creation concerned.

The jury's decisions are final and may not be contested.

## ARTICLE 6: NO PRIZES

The Organising Company may contact certain Participants whose creation has been shortlisted as part of its thinking on the future development of the SKIN EASY range.

Such contact constitutes neither a prize nor a promise to market the creation.
